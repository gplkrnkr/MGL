Trust & Safety

Four layers that protect workers and employers

Trust is not a feature — it is the product. Every element of MGL is designed to make fraud structurally difficult and honest behaviour the path of least resistance.

🔐
Aadhaar-Verified Identity

Every worker is verified against UIDAI's Aadhaar database via OTP. Face liveness detection prevents proxy registration. MGL stores only your Virtual ID — never your raw 12-digit Aadhaar number.

UIDAI AUA Compliant · DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

📞
Contact Masking

Employers never see a worker's phone number until a placement is confirmed. This removes the single biggest motivation for fake employer accounts — contact harvesting — structurally, not just by policy.

Protects 100% of registered workers

⭐
Mutual Rating System

Workers rate employers. Employers rate workers. Neither sees the other's rating until both have submitted — preventing retaliation. Verified placement badges require employer confirmation and cannot be self-awarded.

Inspired by global marketplace trust standards

🤖
AI Fraud Detection

Every account is scored for fraud risk at registration using device fingerprinting, registration speed analysis, and GPS clustering. Bulk fake registrations are detected and held before they enter the worker pool.

Real-time · 24/7 monitoring

✅
Employer GSTIN Verification

Every employer account is verified against the GST API in real time. A fake employer cannot create a job posting with a fabricated GSTIN. Director PAN linkage creates personal accountability behind every employer account.

GST API · Real-time verification

⚖️
ILO Employer Pays Commitment

MGL will never charge a worker a placement fee to secure a job — domestic or overseas. All GCC placement revenue is collected from employers only. Workers pay only their own document costs (₹2,499 total for the full documentation package).

ILO Employer Pays Principle · MEA Policy Aligned
