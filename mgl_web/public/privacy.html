My Grand Labour

Privacy

My Grand Labour is run by MGL Private Limited. This page explains what information the website uses and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you sign in we use your phone number. We also keep what you add yourself, such as your profile, job posts and applications, to run the service. Phone sign-in uses Google Firebase, and reCAPTCHA checks that you are a person. An essential cookie keeps you signed in, and your browser stores small settings such as your job search. The service is hosted on Amazon Web Services in Australia.

Who can see your information

  • Employers: when you apply for a job, that employer sees your name, phone number, skills, MGL worker ID, verification status and the status of your application. Employers can't open your ID documents on our service.
  • Our team: authorised administrators can see uploaded documents and applications, to check them and run the service.
  • Field agents: if an agent registers you, they enter your phone number and name, and may add your skills, location and gender. They can see the profiles they registered.
  • Anyone: open job posts, including the employer's name, are public.
  • Service providers: Amazon Web Services hosts the service, and Google provides phone sign-in and, if you accept, analytics.

ID documents

You may choose to upload a photo, or a copy of an identity document or certificate. This is optional: you can register, search and apply without it. We use documents only to check your details and show a verification status on your profile.

Documents are stored on our server and opened through links that expire. If you upload an Aadhaar copy, please hide the first eight digits (use a masked Aadhaar).

Aadhaar through DigiLocker

This option is not available yet. When it is, it will be your choice, and you will give permission in DigiLocker itself. We will receive your Aadhaar details only to check them, and will keep only that you were verified and when, the last four digits, whether the name matched your profile, and your DigiLocker account ID, so one Aadhaar can't verify several accounts. We will not keep your full Aadhaar number, Aadhaar photo, address or the Aadhaar file. We will update this page before turning it on.

Google Analytics

If you accept, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It sets cookies (names starting with _ga) that can last up to two years, and Google receives details such as the page address, your device and browser type, your approximate location and the site you came from. Google may process this outside India. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

We don't send Google Analytics your name, phone number, profile or documents. We remove search text and other parts after the "?" from page addresses, and it isn't used for advertising.

Your choices and requests

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, withdraw your consent, or name someone to act for you if you die or can't act yourself. Write to contact@mygrandlabour.com from, or mentioning, the phone number on your account. We may need to confirm it is you. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect what was done before, and some services may stop working without that information.
